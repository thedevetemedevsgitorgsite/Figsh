About Me

I’m Ekuyik Sam, known as Figsh. A self-taught full-stack developer from Akwa Ibom, Nigeria, focused on creating tools that feel almost magical because they stay close to the metal area and extreme modularity.

I started by breaking and rebuilding physical toys and circuits. When space ran out, I moved to code. That same curiosity produced tools like the FSCSS - a lightweight CSS preprocessor powering tools across the open-source ecosystem.

with less complexity.

Experience & Journey

Early Creative Experiments

Long before code: doing arts and learning. Curiosity was the only teacher.

Digital Evolution

Shifted from wires to syntax. Experimented with “functions” and Regular Expressions until they became the foundation of FSCSS - released into the wild without a plan, yet still used worldwide.

Open Source Ecosystem

Created the FSCSS core preprocessor, VS Code extension, most of it modules and maintaining the fscss-ttr organization. Continuously shipping improvements (currently v1.2.x).
